Questions

The ones worth asking first.

Does Emma post without asking me?

Only if you tell her to. By default she mails you each draft and posts on your yes. Say "just post" and she posts daily on her own.

Will people know an AI wrote my posts?

The posts go out under your name, about things that are true of you. Emma writes the framing. The moments are yours, which is why she asks you for them.

What do I need?

A LinkedIn account. The tracker runs on ERP•AI, a platform for business apps. If you don't have a workspace there yet, Emma walks you through opening one by mail, then sends you one link to connect LinkedIn.

Where does my data live?

In your own workspace on ERP•AI, the platform the tracker is built on. You can open, edit or export it whenever you like.

Which numbers does it track?

Reactions are read from LinkedIn. Comments, reposts and impressions have fields in the app and are entered by hand for now.
